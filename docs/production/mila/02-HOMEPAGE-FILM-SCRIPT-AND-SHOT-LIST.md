## UGC direction
- Character: witty, confident, warm, curious, slightly teasing; no deceptive claims that she is a live person.
- Video: one recognizable outfit in the hero, natural eye contact, small controlled gestures, single action per shot; avoid “futuristic AI” cliché sequences.
- Mobile: put the entire physical performance in a vertical master; desktop may show *contained video with ambient background*, not cropped head/heels.
- Narration: make a no-audio version fully understandable with captions and visible text. Sound is user-initiated; no claim that browsers will allow unmuted autoplay after gate.
- Edits: cuts on intentional visual beats and approved transitions only; no forced >90s video pre-roll blocking the site.
- Overlay: lip-anchored kiss; centered wordmark. Do not display simulated “money received” as a true payment.
- CTA: “Meet Mila” and “Request Private Studio Access” only when the latter has an owner-verified real destination; otherwise keep existing working in-page actions.
